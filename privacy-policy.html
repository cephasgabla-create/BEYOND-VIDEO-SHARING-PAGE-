Depending on the features you use, information may include your username, account details, profile information, videos, captions, comments, and messages you submit.

Beyond uses Supabase for account authentication and related services. If social sign-in is enabled, the relevant provider may process information under its own privacy policy.

3. Videos and public content

Content you publish may be visible to other visitors. Do not upload private information or content you do not have permission to share. Check your account and content settings before publishing.

4. Storage and service providers

Beyond may use Supabase Storage and database services to store content and account-related data. Hosting and other service providers may process technical information needed to operate and protect the website.

5. How information is used

Information may be used to provide accounts, display videos, support comments and interactions, maintain security, and improve the service.

6. Security and retention

We should apply appropriate access controls and retain information only as needed for legitimate service purposes and applicable legal requirements. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices

You can review the available account settings and contact the website operator to ask about access, correction, or deletion of your information, subject to applicable law.

8. Children and safety

Beyond should be used in accordance with applicable age requirements. Do not use the service to harass others, impersonate people, or share another person's private information without permission.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Beyond website operator using the official contact method provided on the website. Add a monitored contact email here before publishing.

10. Policy updates

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The published version should accurately describe Beyond's actual practices.
