Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This policy explains how Beyond may handle information when you use this video-sharing website. Beyond is being developed, so available features and the information processed may change as the service changes. We will update this page when our practices materially change.

1. Information you provide

Depending on the features you use, you may provide account details such as a username, email address, phone number, profile information, and information you include in messages or support requests. Do not include passwords, verification codes, or other secrets in support messages.

2. Sign-in and service providers

Beyond uses Supabase for authentication and its database and storage services. If you choose a sign-in provider such as Google, that provider may process information under its own privacy policy. Phone sign-in, email verification, and social sign-in may depend on which providers are enabled and configured.

3. Videos and public activity

If you upload a video, choose a profile name or picture, or use social features, the information you submit may be stored by the service. Videos, profile details, comments, likes, follows, or live-room activity may be visible to other users when the relevant feature is available and its settings permit it. Do not upload private information or content you do not have permission to share.

4. How information is used

Information may be used to provide sign-in, operate profiles and video features, display content and interactions, respond to support requests, protect the service from abuse, troubleshoot errors, and maintain security.

5. Storage, security, and retention

Information handled through the configured Supabase project may be stored in its database, authentication system, or file storage. We use the security controls configured for the service, but no website or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Information may remain for as long as needed to operate the service, meet legitimate operational needs, resolve disputes, or comply with applicable law. Actual deletion may depend on the feature and the underlying provider.

6. Sharing

Beyond does not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that help operate the website, shown to other users where you choose to publish it, or disclosed when reasonably necessary to protect users, enforce service rules, or comply with law.

7. Your choices and requests

You can choose what information to submit and should avoid posting anything you want to keep private. To ask about, correct, or request deletion of account information, contact us using the address below. We may need to verify your request and may be unable to remove information that must be retained for legal, security, or operational reasons. Some self-service controls may not yet be available.

8. Children and young users

Beyond is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to Beyond, contact us so we can review the concern. Users should follow the age requirements and laws that apply where they live.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Beyond develops. The date at the top indicates when this page was last updated. Please review this page periodically.

10. Contact Beyond

For privacy questions or requests, email cephas.gabla@mentorswithoutborders.net.

Important: This page describes the intended general handling of information and is not a guarantee that every listed feature is currently available. The published policy should be kept aligned with the actual website, Supabase configuration, and any other services Beyond uses.
